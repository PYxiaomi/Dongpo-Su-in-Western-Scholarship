# Dongpo-Su-in-Western-Scholarship
本仓库收录关于中国文学巨匠苏轼（苏东坡）的优质外文学术资源，含英语及西方研究论文、书籍章节等，聚焦其诗词、散文、人格、仕途、思想与海外影响。   内容涵盖宋代文学、思想史与跨文化研究，呈现与国内传统研究互补的国际视角，供学子、研究者及爱好者参考。所有文献仅限教育与学术用途，为中国古典文学及其海外传播研究提供便利。
This repository is a curated collection of foreign-language academic resources and scholarly studies on Su Shi (Su Dongpo, 1037–1101), one of the most iconic literati in Chinese history. It gathers research articles, papers, book chapters, essays, and critical analyses written in English and other Western languages, focusing on interpretations of Su Shi’s poetry, prose, calligraphy, political thought, philosophical views, life experiences, and cultural legacy from an international perspective.
The materials collected here cover multiple disciplinary dimensions, including classical Chinese literature, comparative literature, intellectual history, Song Dynasty studies, and cross-cultural reception. By organizing and archiving overseas scholarship, this compilation aims to provide researchers, students, and enthusiasts with convenient access to Western interpretations, methodologies, and insights that differ from traditional domestic studies. It seeks to highlight how Su Shi’s literary achievements, personality, and moral spirit have been understood, evaluated, and transmitted in the global academic context.
All resources are compiled for educational and research purposes only, supporting further exploration of Su Shi’s enduring influence across cultures and disciplines. This repository serves as an open reference for anyone studying Song literature, Chinese intellectual history, or the global reception of traditional Chinese culture.
